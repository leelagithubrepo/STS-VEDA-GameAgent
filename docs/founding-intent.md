# VEDA — Founding Intent

VEDA is a new autonomous AI game-playing agent whose first target game is **Slay the Spire**.

Neither the developer nor VEDA assumes prior expertise in how to play or win the game. VEDA must research, understand, and learn the game before attempting autonomous play.

## 1. Research

VEDA researches reliable public material on rules, controls and UI, characters, cards and upgrades, combat mechanics, enemies and intents, buffs/debuffs, relics, potions, map progression, rewards, shops, campfires, events, deck building, pathing, resource management, victory conditions, Ascension, expert strategy, beginner mistakes, and winning principles.

Research uses multiple sources. Disagreement is preserved with its context; advice is never silently converted to a universal rule.

## 2. Build Knowledge

Knowledge is structured and retrievable during play. Each item is one of:

- **game fact** — a verifiable mechanic or rule;
- **strategic principle** — broadly useful guidance;
- **situational strategy** — advice conditional on game state; or
- **hypothesis** — an unvalidated belief.

All externally learned knowledge includes provenance, quotation/paraphrase boundaries, capture time, and confidence. Advice is represented with rationale and conditions rather than as hard-coded behavior.

## 3. Learn by Watching

Expert play is captured as state → choices → decision → reasoning (stated or inferred) → resulting state/outcome. These are examples for comparison, not button-by-button scripts.

## 4. Begin Playing

After foundational knowledge is sufficient, VEDA starts with **Ironclad, Ascension 0**. Her operating loop is:

**Observe → Understand → Retrieve → Determine legal choices → Reason → Decide → Act → Verify → Learn.**

## 5. Learn From Experience

Important decisions retain pre-state, choices, choice made, reasoning, prediction, observed result, immediate outcome, combat outcome, and run outcome. Predictions are compared with outcomes. Repeated evidence—not one win or loss—changes confidence in strategic beliefs.

## Objective

VEDA becomes capable through **research + observation + reasoning + experimentation + experience + evaluation**. Success is measurable by win rate, progression, decision quality, and improvement. At any point VEDA should be able to explain what she believes, why, its provenance, and how experience changed it.
