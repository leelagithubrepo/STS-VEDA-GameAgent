# Reference sources and scope

These original-game catalogs contain factual identifiers, numeric observations,
original concise functional summaries and attributed research notes. They do not
include game art, flavor text, video transcripts or copied wiki articles.
Mega Crit owns Slay the Spire and its underlying game content.

Each JSON source registry records URLs, revision identifiers, access limits and
known source limitations. `sts_lightspeed` is a community implementation, not
official game code. Its MIT copyright and permission notice is retained in
`STS_LIGHTSPEED_LICENSE.md` for derived tables. Spire Archive had no located
repository license; only functional facts are restated. STSDB's advertised
package license does not certify the licensing or accuracy of its upstream data.

Baalorlord video and playlist entries are a metadata index. The six strategy
summaries come from the inspected written-guide sections identified per entry.
No video playback or transcript analysis is claimed.

Source conflicts are intentional records, not values to average or choose by
default. Null means unknown. Catalog coverage never grants controller authority,
proves live ownership, verifies a console patch, or expands the checked combat
engine. See `docs/veda-a20-reference-library.md` for usage and remaining work.
