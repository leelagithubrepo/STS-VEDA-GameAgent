# VEDA franchise contract

VEDA is the reusable franchise and platform for player-facing game advisors.
It supplies the shared philosophy and capabilities: observe, understand,
advise, predict, verify, remember, and learn.

Game mechanics, strategy, telemetry interpretations, and accomplishments stay
with their own game advisor. They must not be represented as VEDA-wide facts.

Current implementation: **Game Advisor #1 — Slay the Spire Advisor**.
Future games add a new game-advisor contract; they do not redefine VEDA.
